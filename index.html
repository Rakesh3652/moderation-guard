How this score is computed: when the AI model is available, each of the first 6 categories is scored directly by a fine-tuned BERT toxicity classifier (Xenova/toxic-bert) running in your browser. Self-Harm Encouragement always uses a dedicated high-recall keyword check, kept rule-based intentionally so this safety-critical category never depends solely on a single model's confidence. Threat and Self-Harm Encouragement scores are multiplied ×1.3 before a weighted-max aggregation produces the final severity score (Safe 0–40% · Mild 40–70% · Toxic 70–90% · Critical 90–100%).
